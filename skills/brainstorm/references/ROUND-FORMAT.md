# Round format

Head each round with its phase and its number within that phase (`What · Round 2`, `How · Round 1`), adding the kind in the first round that follows its choice and in the round after it changes (`What · Round 1 · Decision`). Group the round's questions under **category** headings, each a short label you pick for what its questions share (`Scope`, `Data model`, `Error handling`), and close each category with a horizontal rule. Head each question with its number and title, counting on across categories, then ask it, list its options, and mark your recommendation. Tag a question that reopens a `What` branch in a `How` round `(What)`, after its title. When a question has no natural options, give your recommended answer without letters. Word each question so "ok" accepts your recommendation. Write each round in plain prose, by [PROSE.md](../../brain/references/PROSE.md), each question asking one decision. Done when each sentence passes PROSE.md's rules 3 and 4, checked before you send.

```
# What · Round 1

## 🔷 <category>

### ❓ Q1 - <question title>

<the question>

- **A** - <option>
- **B** - <option>

✅ **B** - <your recommendation and the one-line reason>

### ❓ Q2 - <question title>

<the question>

- **A** - <option>
- **B** - <option>

✅ **A** - <your recommendation and the one-line reason>

---

## 🔷 <another category>

### ❓ Q3 - <question title>

<the question>

- **A** - <option>
- **B** - <option>
- **C** - <option>

✅ **A** - <your recommendation and the one-line reason>

---

...

---

Reply with letters (`1B 2A`), `ok` to take every recommendation, or your own answer.
```
